How these were picked

The search: three or more bedrooms for rent on the Miami MLS in Downtown and Brickell only (Edgewater was excluded to stay close to the Metro), up to $5,600 a month. That gave 82 listings, compared with 4 in the original saved search. Houses and duplexes were removed, and so were most buildings without clear amenities, anything with a rent above $5,200, and units already under contract.

Prices are the MLS asking rent on October 12. Ask each landlord about free months or other concessions. Only unit #3 is fully furnished; a furniture rental package would add about $690 a month to the others. Every listing shows a one-year lease.

Brickell (#1, #2, #6). Neo Vertika sits just south of the Miami River beside Brickell City Centre, about a 7-minute walk to the Brickell Metrorail and Metromover station. Vue at Brickell is on the south side of the same station, only a few blocks away, with Brickell Avenue restaurants nearby.

North bank of the river (#5). Wind is at South Miami Avenue and the river, a short walk across the bridge to Brickell City Centre and the station.

Downtown (#3, #4). 50 Biscayne and The Loft are north of the river near Bayfront Park and Bayside Marketplace, close to the Metromover loop. For Metrorail, ride Metromover to Brickell or Government Center.

All five locations are within about 1.5 miles of each other, so they can be toured in one trip.

The shortlist

Every building here is in Downtown or Brickell. Chedi does not need pets, so pet rules were not used to pick. For every unit we still need the exact move-in date and whether a guarantor or prepaid rent is accepted.
