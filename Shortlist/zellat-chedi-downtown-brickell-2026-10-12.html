How these were picked

The search: three or more bedrooms for rent on the Miami MLS in Downtown and Brickell only (Edgewater was excluded to stay close to the Metro), up to $5,600 a month. That gave 82 listings, compared with 4 in the original saved search. Houses and duplexes were removed, and so were most buildings without clear amenities, anything with a rent above $5,200, and units already under contract.

Prices for #1 through #6 are the MLS asking rent on October 12. #7 and #8 are base rents from the Zillow building pages on October 15, before fees. Ask each landlord about free months or other concessions. Only unit #3 is confirmed furnished; a furniture rental package would add about $690 a month to the others. MLS listings show one-year leases.

Brickell (#1, #2, #6). Neo Vertika sits just south of the Miami River beside Brickell City Centre, about a 7-minute walk to the Brickell Metrorail and Metromover station. Vue at Brickell is a few blocks south of the same station, with Brickell Avenue restaurants nearby.

River edge (#5). Wind is at South Miami Avenue and the river, a short walk across the bridge to Brickell City Centre and the station.

Downtown core (#3, #4, #7). 50 Biscayne and The Loft are near Bayfront Park and Bayside Marketplace, close to the Metromover loop. Downtown 5th is on NE 5th Street with Metromover at its door and is about a quarter mile (straight line) from the Government Center station.

North Downtown (#8). Melody Tower is at NE 14th Street, about a mile north of Government Center and the farthest pick from the Brickell station. It is near Edgewater, so confirm the location works for Chedi. The nearest Metromover stop was not measured.

#1 through #6 are within about 1.5 miles of each other. Melody Tower is another mile or so north, so touring all eight is best done in two stops: Brickell and Downtown first, then Melody Tower.
