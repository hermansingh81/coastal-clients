How these were picked

Must-haves: 3 bedrooms, 2+ bathrooms, furnished, between South Miami and Downtown, $4,300 or less, and a pet policy that allows a dog. The first screen ran in the Miami MLS and returned 4 results; none was a clean fit. The rest come from Zillow, with furnished status and pet rules read off each listing page.

Applying without income: houses and duplexes are rented by individual owners, who can say yes to a parent guarantor or several months paid in advance. Condo buildings run board approval and usually will not. That is why this list leans toward houses and small buildings.

Availability changes daily. Each card says whether the listing is active and what still needs a call. Rents shown are asking prices.

The shortlist

Start with the first three. They are under $4,000, accept dogs and read as 12-month rentals. The Coconut Grove villa is the one to push on price.
