How these were picked

Must-haves: 3 bedrooms inside the green area Chedi marked, close to a Metrorail station for the commute, with building amenities such as a pool and gym, and asking rent of $4,000 a month or less. Houses and duplexes were left out.

Prices are the asking rent from each building's public listing on October 9. Ask each landlord about free months or other concessions. None are furnished. A furniture rental package would add about $690 a month. Distances marked as estimates are measured from the street address.

Coconut Grove (#1, #2). Grove Station and Gibraltar are on SW 27th Avenue, north of the Coconut Grove Metrorail station (blue square). Grove Station is about 0.2 mile from the station and Gibraltar about 0.3 mile. CocoWalk, the bay and the Grove restaurants are a short drive south.

Between the Grove and Coral Gables (#4, #5). Gables Marquis and Keystone Villas sit along SW 22nd Terrace and Coral Way, west of 27th Avenue. Neither is walkable to the train, so plan on a short drive or bus to Coconut Grove or Douglas Road station.

Coral Gables (#3). Villa Majorca is the farthest west, west of Douglas Road near Granada Boulevard, in Coral Gables. It is quiet and leafy but the longest trip to the train.

The shortlist

New on October 12: #4 and #5 come from the Miami MLS and link to their Coastal pages. The first three are leased by the building managers and are not on the MLS, so their links go to the Apartments.com building page. Grove Station and Gibraltar are both on 27th Avenue next to the Coconut Grove Metrorail station, so they can be toured on the same trip. For each one we still need to confirm the move-in date, lease length, and whether a guarantor or prepaid rent is accepted.
