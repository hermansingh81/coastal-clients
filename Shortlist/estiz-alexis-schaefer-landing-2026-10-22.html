How we're screening these

Cap Rate = (Annual Rent − Annual Taxes − Annual Common Charges) / Purchase Price Unlevered — financing excluded so every unit compares apples-to-apples. Insurance, management fees and a vacancy/repair allowance are not included; expect this to run roughly 0.5–1.0 point lower once those are layered in on a specific deal.

Rent is a same-building comp estimate, not the asking or prior-tenant rent: the combined North + South 2BR rental comp set has a median of $7,250/mo, which also matches #20D's own current in-contract rent almost exactly — a good sanity check. 18E, 15A and 17D use this figure; #18D uses its own confirmed $7,400/mo achieved rent instead. Each card below shows the specific rental comps behind its number.

Common charges for 18E and 15A are the listings’ own published figures (~$1,657 and $1,655/mo); the off-market units are modeled at the same $/sqft rate, since the development is one JV complex across both towers.

Taxes today are near-zero across the board ($6–7/mo) under the building’s 421-a abatement, which runs through 2032 — the confirmed end of the phase-out taper, with 2033 being the first full market-rate tax year. Taxes after 2032 are a screening-level estimate at 1.0% of price/year (NYC market-rate condos typically run 0.6%–1.3%); every cap rate below is shown both ways so there’s no surprise in the economics.

Building risk: the assessment and the abatement

421-a tax abatement — expires 2032

Confirmed directly on both active listings (18E, 15A): 421-a abatement in effect until 2032, the end of the phase-out taper — 2033 is the first full market-rate tax year. Current taxes run $6–7/mo across the units checked, effectively nominal. This is the single biggest swing factor in the numbers above, which is why every cap rate is shown both at today’s abated tax and at the post-2032 estimate.

Special assessment — South confirmed, North confirmed too

446 Kent (South tower): $299–$545/month, in effect through 2029, for capital improvements/building renovations. South has zero active listings right now, so this isn’t live-relevant to the four prospects, but it’s useful context on the building’s overall capital condition.

440 Kent (North tower, where all four prospects sit): an earlier, smaller North assessment has lapsed, but a currently-active assessment of approx. $220/month through end of 2030 is confirmed for #17D and built into its numbers (it ends before the abatement fully expires, so it drops out of the post-2032 figure). Whether this same assessment applies to 18E, 15A and 18D is still being confirmed.

Negotiation read

18E (4.62% at ask) is the strongest number on the table and the newest listing (6 DOM) — least negotiating room on price, but the cleanest comp. 17D at the modeled $1.5M (4.33%) comfortably beats 18D's $1.58M ask (4.39%) on identical assumptions — the two off-market deals aren’t equivalent even though they’re the same line. 15A (3.78%) is the weakest of the four on cap rate despite being relisted after a price cut and sitting 24 days — DOM and the recent $25K reduction are the lever there, not the underlying economics.
For 18D specifically: the North-tower closed-comp set above is the case to bring back to Alexis’s friend — recent same-line D-unit sales (#8D at $1,240,000 recorded closing 8/31/26, #9D delisted at $1,450,000 asking 10/19/26) sit well under $1.58M, and neither #18E nor #15A at their own asking prices are priced anywhere close to $1.58M for a comparable or larger unit. That’s the comp argument to negotiate 18D down with, simultaneous to bidding 18E/15A and firming up 17D.
