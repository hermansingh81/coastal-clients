How these were picked and how rent was calculated

Must-haves: 3 bedrooms, a gym and a pool in the building, furnished, inside the Wynwood, Edgewater and Downtown area marked on the map, and an average of no more than $4,500 a month over 12 months after concessions (per Chedi, Oct 5). Pets are no longer a requirement. Alea and ParkLine are the two that may offer furnished units; the rest still need to be asked. Guarantors or prepaid rent must also be accepted: 10X, which does not, is out.

Effective rent: asking rent × (12 months minus the free months) ÷ 12. For example, $4,850 with 3 free months is $3,638 a month over a 12-month lease. Most buildings advertise “up to” 2-3 months on select homes, so each number needs to be confirmed in writing for the exact unit. Zillow's total price may also include required monthly fees.

Applying without income: large buildings usually ask for about 3 times the rent in income. With a parent guarantor or prepaid rent the application is usually still possible, but each leasing office has to confirm.

The shortlist

Chedi's tour list comes first: Alea, ParkLine, Flow and Modera Riverside. 10X Miami River was dropped because it accepts no guarantors or prepaid rent. He can tour any day and asked for advance notice. For each one we still need to confirm that the unit is furnished, that a guarantor or prepaid rent is accepted, and that the 12-month average stays at or under $4,500.
