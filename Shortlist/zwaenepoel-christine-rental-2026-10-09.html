How these were picked

Must-haves: 2+ bedrooms, 1,200+ sq ft, unfurnished, a community pool, built 2015 or later, 12-month lease, a dog allowed, and downtown, Brickell, Edgewater or Midtown. Listings came from a saved MLS search and a hand-picked MLS cart, then were checked one by one. Furnished units, listings that say no pets and buildings older than 2015 were dropped.

Corner units: a unit is marked corner only where the MLS listing says so. Those come first. The last three are not stated as corners, so the floor plan decides, and we will check it before touring. The two corner units at Paramount are furnished and are listed under Also found.

The dog: their file shows one dog, a 15 kg (33 lb) Eurasier. Most of these buildings allow dogs with a size limit and a pet fee, and the MLS records do not give the weight limit. Each building's rule is to be confirmed before a showing. Lease length and the mid-November move-in date are also to be confirmed with each listing agent. Unit 1 is available November 18.

Start with the first three. DUA Brickell is the lowest rent and is available November 18, 801 S Miami Ave has the bay-facing wraparound balcony, and the Brickell Flatiron corner is a good fit once its pet rule is confirmed.
