How these were picked

Must-haves: 2+ bedrooms, 1,200+ sq ft, unfurnished, a community pool, built 2015 or later, 12-month lease, a dog allowed, and downtown, Brickell, Edgewater or Midtown. The search ran across every active rental in zips 33131, 33132, 33137 and 33127 up to $11,500, then dropped furnished units and any listing that says no pets. At Paramount, only corner units were kept, and none of them is unfurnished.

The dog: their file shows one dog, a 15 kg (33 lb) Eurasier. Most of these buildings allow dogs with a size limit and a pet fee, and the MLS records do not give the weight limit. Each building's rule is to be confirmed before a showing.

Living room and corner units: only the Brickell Flatiron and Gran Paraiso listings say corner. For the others, the floor plan decides, and we will check it before touring. Lease length and the mid-November move-in date are not stated on the MLS records, so we will confirm both with each listing agent.

The shortlist

Start with the first three. Casa Bella is the newest and largest per bedroom, and the Brickell Flatiron unit is a corner at $7,500 once its pet rule is confirmed.
