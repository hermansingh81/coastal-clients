The short answer

Yes, very likely. Comparable two-bedroom, two-bath homes of similar size in Mid and North Beach rent for about $4,250 a month. Whether that beats owning depends mainly on whether you still have a mortgage.

  • If you have a mortgage: owning likely costs you about $4,350–$4,700 a month all-in, so renting is about the same or less, and you would also free up roughly $273K in cash.
  • If the home is paid off: owning costs about $2,440 a month (HOA, taxes, insurance). Renting costs about $1,800 more, but about $610K invested at 3.6% or better covers that difference. At 4% you come out about $225 a month ahead.
